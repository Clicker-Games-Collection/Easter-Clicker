## How to Play

Begin your egg-crafting journey by tapping on the giant egg on your screen. Each click earns you points, which you can use to unlock automatic egg generators that produce eggs effortlessly. As your production increases, spend your points wisely to acquire more eggs and enhance your crafting capabilities.

## Key Features

- **Engaging Clicker Mechanics**: Tap to generate points and craft vibrant Easter eggs.
- **Automatic Production**: Unlock generators that produce eggs automatically, allowing you to focus on upgrades.
- **Point Management**: Strategically allocate your points to maximize egg production and efficiency.
